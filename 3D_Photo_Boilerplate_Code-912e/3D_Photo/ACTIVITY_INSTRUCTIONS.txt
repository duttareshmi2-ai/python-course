3D PHOTO (AI Expert, Module 5, Lesson 4)

WHAT YOU WILL BUILD
A live camera app with two halves. Left: your camera. Right: a map of how far away the AI thinks every pixel is
(near = bright yellow, far = dark purple). Press SPACE and your photo starts to wobble like a 3D picture, and a short
video of it is saved next to the program.

THE AI
Depth Anything V2 Small, a small AI model (depth.onnx, 27 MB). It looks at ONE ordinary picture and guesses, for every
pixel, how near it is. It learned this from millions of photos. It runs on your own computer: no internet, no key.

YOUR FILE
photo_3d.py. Lines marked (given) are done for you: read their notes A to G. You write TODO 1 to TODO 4, in order.
The TODO comments explain each job in words. Run the program after each TODO and check the result.

STEP 1: Run the starter (setup: see SETUP.txt)
The camera shows on the left, but the right half is black. Press SPACE: the "3D photo" does not move, and its depth
map is black too. Why? Read depth() and wobble().

STEP 2: TODO 1, depth(): get the AI's raw answer
Shrink the picture (width a multiple of 14), turn it into the numbers the model learned on, and run the model.
Check: press SPACE. The right half is no longer black, but it looks wrong (stripes, or a mostly white patch).

STEP 3: TODO 2, depth(): numbers from 0 (far) to 1 (near)
Check: press SPACE. The depth map is smooth: near things bright yellow, far things dark purple.

STEP 4: TODO 3, wobble(): make the photo move
Check: press SPACE. The photo wobbles: near and far things slide opposite ways, the middle distance stays still.
Look at the edges of near things: they stretch. Why?

STEP 5: TODO 4, keep_measuring(): the AI in the background
Check: the right half shows a live depth map while you move, and the camera picture stays smooth.

STEP 6: Find what the AI cannot do
Point the camera at a photo or a poster on a screen or wall, at a mirror or a window, and at a plain white wall.
What does the depth map say each time, and is it right?

KEYS
SPACE = make a 3D photo (and back to live)    q or Esc = quit (or close the window)

TIPS
Sit about an arm's length from the camera, with things at different distances behind you. Making a 3D photo takes a
few seconds. Errors appear in the terminal, not in the camera window. An error in the background AI does not stop
the program: the right half just stays black, and the error is in the terminal.

SHOW YOUR PARENT
Make a 3D photo with something near the camera and the room behind it, play the saved video, and say: "The AI
guessed the distance of every pixel from one picture, and my code moves near and far pixels differently."
