LIVING PAINTING: where the AI models come from, and their licences

candy_style.onnx, mosaic_style.onnx, udnie_style.onnx, rain-princess_style.onnx (keys 1 to 4)
  Fast Neural Style Transfer, ONNX model zoo (https://github.com/onnx/models, validated/vision/style_transfer/fast_neural_style,
  opset 9 files), converted from PyTorch examples (https://github.com/pytorch/examples/tree/main/fast_neural_style).
  Licence: BSD-3-Clause, Copyright (c) 2017, Pytorch contributors (LICENSE-fast-neural-style-BSD-3-Clause.txt).
  Each model learned one style from one painting or picture. Renamed only (for example candy-9.onnx -> candy_style.onnx).

any_style_transfer.onnx (keys 5 to 9)
  The "style transfer" part of Google Magenta's arbitrary image stylization model
  (style_transfer_f16_384.tflite, from https://storage.googleapis.com/download.tensorflow.org/models/tflite/arbitrary_style_transfer/),
  Copyright The Magenta Team, Apache License 2.0 (LICENSE-Magenta-Apache-2.0.txt).
  Changed by Codingal: converted from TFLite to ONNX with tf2onnx 1.17, and the picture size made free (not only 384x384).

style_vectors.npz
  100 numbers per style, made once with Magenta's style prediction model (style_predict_f16_256.tflite, Apache License 2.0)
  from public-domain paintings listed in STYLE_SOURCES.json (Wikimedia Commons). The paintings themselves are not included.

modnet.onnx (the cut-out, as in Lesson 2)
  MODNet portrait matting model, ONNX export from https://huggingface.co/Xenova/modnet (tagged Apache-2.0 there).
  Original MODNet: https://github.com/ZHKKKe/MODNet, Apache License 2.0 (LICENSE-MODNet-Apache-2.0.txt).
