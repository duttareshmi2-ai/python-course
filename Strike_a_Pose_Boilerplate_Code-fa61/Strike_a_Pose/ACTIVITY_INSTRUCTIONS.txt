STRIKE A POSE (AI Expert, Module 5, Lesson 5)

WHAT YOU WILL BUILD
A body game for your camera. A pose appears on the screen, like "T-pose!" or "Arms up!". Copy it with your body and
hold it for 1 second: "Nice!" flashes, you score a point and the game saves a photo. Five poses, 10 seconds each.

THE AI
MoveNet Lightning, a small AI model (movenet.onnx) from Google, trained on photos of people moving. For one person it
answers with 17 body points (nose, eyes, ears, shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees, ankles). Each point is
(y, x, how sure), all from 0 to 1. It runs on your own computer.

YOUR FILE
strike_a_pose.py. Lines marked (given) are done for you: read their notes A to H. You write TODO 1 to TODO 4, in order.
The TODO comments explain each job in words. Run the program after each TODO and check the result.

STEP 1: Run the starter (setup: see SETUP.txt)
The game runs: a pose name, a timer, a stick figure of the pose to copy. But no skeleton appears on you, the bar always
says "match 0%", and the end screen says "You scored 0 of 5!". Why? Read find_body().

STEP 2: TODO 1, find_body(): get the 17 points from the AI
Pad the frame to a square, resize to 192 x 192, change BGR to RGB as int32, add the front axis: (1, 192, 192, 3).
Run the model and turn each (y, x, sure) into (x in pixels, y in pixels, sure).
Check: stand in front of the camera. A yellow skeleton follows your body. (The bar still says "match 0%": TODO 2.)

STEP 3: TODO 2, match(): compare angles, the short way round
Skip unsure arms (less than 0.3 sure). Find each angle's difference to the target the short way round the circle.
Score = 100 minus the mean difference, never below 0.
Check: the bar says "match 10%" whatever you do. The angles are still placeholders: TODO 3 fixes that.

STEP 4: TODO 3, arm_angles(): from points to angles
For each arm bone (shoulder to elbow, elbow to wrist), the angle is np.arctan2(how far down, how far right), in degrees.
Check: the match % follows your arms. Copy the pose: it goes over 80% and the bar turns green.

STEP 5: TODO 4: hold it to score
Remember when the match first reached 80%. After 1 second held: score + 1, "Nice!", save a photo, next pose.
When the time is up: next pose, no point.
Check: hold a pose for 1 second. "Nice!" flashes, the score goes up and a pose_ photo appears in this folder.

STEP 6: Find what the AI cannot do
Hide one arm behind your back. Stand so close that your arms leave the picture. Let a friend step in next to you.
Stand sideways. What happens each time, and why?

KEYS
r = play again    q or Esc = quit (or close the window)

TIPS
Step back so your head, shoulders, elbows and hands are all in the picture. Good light helps the AI be sure.
Errors appear in the terminal, not in the camera window.

SHOW YOUR PARENT
Score 5 of 5, and say: "The AI finds 17 points on my body, and my code turns my arms into angles and checks them
against the pose."
