STRIKE A POSE: SETUP

1. Keep this folder whole: strike_a_pose.py and movenet.onnx (the AI model, 9 MB) must stay together.
2. Open a terminal INSIDE this folder and install the three packages once:
       python -m pip install -r requirements.txt
   (on Mac or Linux you may need python3 instead of python). Use the same Python that runs the program.
3. Run:
       python strike_a_pose.py
4. A window called "Strike a Pose" opens with your camera. Click on it once, so it gets your key presses.
5. Step back until the camera sees you from your head to your waist, with both arms in view.

Needs: Python 3.10 or newer and a webcam. No internet is needed after the install. Nothing you film leaves your computer.
The game saves a pose_<time>.jpg photo each time you score. They stay in this folder: delete them when you are done.
If the window closes at once, read the terminal: the error is there.
Camera busy or blocked? Close Zoom, Teams or the camera app. On Windows: Settings > Privacy > Camera, allow apps to use the camera.
